# 1️⃣ Problem Clarity (Before You Build)

☐ Who is the exact target user? (Be specific: “2nd year CS student preparing for RGPV DSA exams,” not “students.”)

☐ What are the top 2–3 painful problems they face?

☐ Which problem is most painful? Rank them.

☐ What is the current workaround? Why is it insufficient?

☐ What measurable impact does this problem cause? (Time wasted? Money lost? Frustration?)

If you cannot articulate pain clearly, do not build yet.

---

# 2️⃣ Outcome Definition (Value Clarity)

☐ What is the primary transformation for the user?
(“From confused about rate limiting → confidently simulating and visualizing algorithms.”)

☐ What does success look like in one sentence?

☐ Is the benefit tangible and observable?

If the benefit feels vague, refine it.

---

# 3️⃣ Solution Discipline

☐ How does your product directly solve each listed problem?

☐ What is the core mechanism (not features)?

☐ What are you not building right now?

☐ Can you explain how it works in 5 steps or fewer?

If you need too many features to justify value, you are overbuilding.

---

# 4️⃣ Strategic Intent

☐ Why does this problem matter now?

☐ Why are you building this?

☐ What larger direction could this evolve into?

---

# 5️⃣ User Reality Check

☐ Can you write a believable customer quote?

☐ Does it sound like a real human describing a real pain?

☐ Would someone actually say this?

If the quote feels artificial, your understanding of the user is shallow.
